# Play Panda — "Birthday Packages" Website Page Spec

**Purpose:** Build a conversion-focused Birthday Packages page for theplaypanda.com.
**Primary goal:** Turn enquiries into venue visits and bookings (our data shows enquiries come in but don't convert — this page is built to fix that).
**Audience:** Parents planning a child's birthday, mostly browsing on mobile.

> Note for build: the matching designed menu (JPG/PDF) is the visual reference. This doc is the source of truth for copy, structure, and behaviour. Keep all prices visible — for parents, clarity converts.

---

## 1. Page meta (SEO)

- **URL slug:** `/birthday-parties` (or `/birthdays`)
- **Page title (H1):** Birthday Packages
- **Title tag:** Kids' Birthday Party Packages in Bandlaguda Jagir, Hyderabad | Play Panda
- **Meta description:** Host your child's birthday at Play Panda — 10,000 sq ft of screen-free play, a dedicated coordinator, and décor packages for groups up to 70. One price for kids & adults. Book a visit today.
- **Primary keywords:** kids birthday party Hyderabad, birthday party venue Bandlaguda Jagir, indoor play area birthday, kids party packages.

## 2. Section-by-section content & layout

Sections in order, top to bottom. Mobile-first; all CTAs lead to WhatsApp or the enquiry form.

### 2.1 Hero (coral background, festive)
- **H1:** Birthday Packages
- **Tagline:** A big celebration inside a little one's world.
- **Sub-line (pill/badge):** One price for your whole group — kids & adults included
- **Visuals:** white Play Panda logo, mascot (cap + sunglasses), bunting across the top, scalloped bottom edge.
- **Primary CTA button:** `Enquire on WhatsApp` → opens WhatsApp chat (see §6).
- **Secondary CTA:** `Book a Visit` → scrolls to form / opens form modal.

### 2.2 Every party includes (band, light/cream background)
Heading: **Every Play Panda party includes**
- Dedicated party coordinator
- All 9 play zones
- Themed e-invite
- 3-hour celebration slot

Sub-line: *Your dedicated coordinator manages vendors, timings & flow — so you can simply relax and enjoy the day.*

> The coordinator line is a key selling point (effortless / everything handled) — give it visual weight, don't bury it.

### 2.3 Packages (3 cards)
Heading: **Choose your celebration**
Three cards side-by-side on desktop, stacked on mobile. **Grand is the highlighted "Most Popular" card** (coral border + ribbon, slightly raised). Each card shows two prices: Weekday and Weekend.

See full pricing in §3. Card copy:

- **CLASSIC** — *Up to 20 guests* — **Welcome board**
  Space, play & a dedicated coordinator for an intimate celebration. Add décor any time.
- **GRAND** (Most Popular) — *Up to 40 guests* — **Welcome board + standard décor**
  A bright, joyful setup, with a coordinator keeping the day running smoothly.
- **FAIRYTALE** — *Up to 70 guests* — **Welcome board + themed décor**
  Their world brought to life — plus a Play Panda surprise gift for the birthday star.

### 2.4 Gallery & proof  *(RECOMMENDED — high impact on conversion)*
A grid of real party photos + a short highlight reel + 2–3 parent testimonials (name + child's age).
> This is the single biggest conversion lever for a newer venue. Parents won't risk a birthday on a place they haven't seen a party happen at. Prioritise adding real photos/video here as they're captured.

### 2.5 Your food, your way (band)
Heading: **Your food, your way**
Body: Bring your own caterer & cake, or choose from our trusted partner caterers. Café snacks & beverages available on the day.
> Food is intentionally NOT included in the package price. Don't display per-plate food pricing on this page.

### 2.6 Add-ons (section)
Heading: **Make it extra**
Intro: Add a little extra magic — choose any of these for your celebration.
List the add-ons (see §4 for the price reference). Group as: **Experiences**, **Photography & Reels**, **Décor upgrades**.

### 2.7 How booking works (simple 3–4 step strip)
1. **Enquire** — WhatsApp or call us with your date.
2. **Visit** — come see the space and lock your theme (this is where most bookings happen — push the visit).
3. **Reserve** — 50% advance secures your date.
4. **Celebrate** — show up and enjoy; we handle the rest.

### 2.8 FAQ  *(RECOMMENDED — helps SEO + removes objections)*
Suggested questions: What's included in the price? Is food included? How many guests can attend? Can adults come too? Do you provide décor and themes? Can I bring my own caterer/cake? How do I hold a date? What are the time slots? Is the play area safe and supervised?
(Answers pull from this doc + Terms.)

### 2.9 Terms & fine print (collapsible/accordion)
See §5. Keep it accessible but not alarming — lead with reassurance.

### 2.10 Final CTA / contact footer (coral)
- **Heading:** Let's plan their big day.
- **Sub:** Book a visit or reserve your date — we'll handle the rest.
- **Contact:** Call / WhatsApp +91 90638 17084 · theplaypanda.com · 3rd Floor, Bandlaguda Jagir
- Embed a Google Map; add a sticky "WhatsApp us" button on mobile.

## 5. Terms & conditions (condensed for web)

**Reservations**
- Dates confirmed only on payment of 50% advance; slots can't be held without it.
- Finalise guest count, menu and add-ons at least 3 days before the party.
- Guest count includes adults and children. Babies under 1 are not counted.
- If guest count rises >20% on the day, extra guests are charged at 25% above the per-guest rate.
- Parties begin at the booked time; extra time ₹2,000 per 30 mins (subject to availability).
- Private/exclusive party minimum billing: Weekday ₹60K, Weekend ₹75K (base package only; GST + add-ons extra).

**Food, décor & activities**
- Outside food & beverages not permitted, except birthday cake and cupcakes.
- In-house themed décor, return favours, photography and activities available.
- External vendor corkage: décor ₹2,500 · photographer ₹2,000 · activity vendor ₹1,000.
- No alcohol anywhere on the premises.
- Play Panda may use party photos/videos for marketing unless told otherwise in writing.

**Payment & cancellation**
- Full balance settled before the event; add-ons/socks billed same day after.
- 5% GST on all packages. Advance is non-refundable.
- Cancellation 7+ days before: 50% charged. Within 7 days: 100% charged.
- Rescheduling allowed up to 7 days before (₹10,000 fee, subject to availability).
- For 40+ guests, valet recommended at ₹1,500.

**Safety**
- Team supervises and supports; adult supervision recommended for under-5s.
- Socks mandatory in play areas (available at reception). Footwear/bags kept outside.
- Main guest responsible for any damage during the event.

## 7. Brand & design tokens

**Colours (hex):**
- Primary Coral `#FF613A`
- Green `#009D72` · Yellow `#FFBB01` · Purple `#8061BE` · Teal `#01C9C7` · Pink `#FF7EBE` · Terracotta `#D06F28`
- Ink/Text `#241C16` · Background cream `#FFF7F0` · White `#FFFFFF`
- Card tints: Classic teal `#D3F2F1` · Grand highlight cream `#FFFDEE` + coral border · Fairytale purple `#E7DFF7`

**Typography:**
- Brand font: **Hank Rnd** — Black/Bold for headings, Regular/Light for body.
- If Hank Rnd isn't web-licensed, use a rounded geometric fallback: **Fredoka**, **Baloo 2**, or **Quicksand** (Google Fonts). Headings bold/extra-bold, body regular.

**Brand devices (use to match the deck):**
- Scalloped / cloud edges between coloured sections
- Bunting (triangular flags) for festive headers
- Confetti dots, sparkles, rounded squiggles as accents
- Rounded "pill" shapes for tags/buttons; pastel tint cards with generous corner radius
- Mascot (cap + sunglasses panda), used as 3D render and flat illustration

**Tone of voice:** warm, playful, premium, child-centric. Short sentences. Lead with the experience, not the price.

## 9. Conversion checklist (why this page is built this way)
- [ ] Prices clearly visible (B2C → clarity converts; hidden prices lose parents)
- [ ] One headline price for the whole group (kills sticker shock + the adults problem)
- [ ] Gallery + testimonials present (proof is the #1 lever for a newer venue)
- [ ] WhatsApp is the primary CTA (that's where enquiries already come from)
- [ ] "Book a Visit" pushed hard (visiting is what closes parties)
- [ ] Coordinator framed as "effortless / handled for you"
- [ ] Mobile-first, fast loading, sticky contact bar
- [ ] FAQ answers objections before they email
