## Section 11 — FAQ

**Layout:** Accordion (expand/collapse). Good for SEO and reduces low-quality enquiries.

**How much do I need to invest?**
Indicative fit-out is around ₹1,500–2,000 per sq. ft. Total investment depends on format — roughly ₹40 L for a compact Express centre up to ₹2 Cr+ for a Flagship — plus a one-time franchise fee.

**Do I need prior experience running a play centre?**
No prior play-centre experience is required. We look for hands-on partners who can run a service business and genuinely care about kids and families.

**How long does it take to open?**
Typically a few months from signing — covering site finalisation, build-out and team training — before your grand opening. *(Confirm exact timeline once finalised.)*

**What support will I receive?**
A brand licence, the full operations playbook, site-selection help, design and fit-out guidance, team training, and launch marketing support.

**Do I need to set up a kitchen?**
No — food and beverage is part of the model and supported by us, so you won't need to run a full commercial kitchen.

**Which cities are you expanding to?**
We're open to metros, growing cities and tier-2/3 towns across India. Share your location in the enquiry form and we'll take it from there.

## Brand & design notes

**Colours (hex)**
- Yellow `#FFBB01` (primary) · Coral `#FF613A` · Purple `#8061BE` · Teal `#01C9C9` · Green `#009D72` · Pink `#FF7DBC` · Brown `#D46E24`
- For large yellow fills, use a softer tint (e.g. `#FFEFC0`) to avoid a harsh look.

**Typography**
- Headings: Hank Rnd (brand head font); Body: Hank Rnd Regular/Light. Use a rounded web-safe fallback if the brand font isn't licensed for web.

**Logo usage**
- On light backgrounds: primary logo (coral wordmark + black/white panda).
- On dark/purple backgrounds: the white "sticker" outline version with the wordmark knocked out and the panda kept black-and-white. Never fill the whole logo (including the panda) in a single solid colour.

**Tone of voice:** Warm, playful and family-friendly, but premium and trustworthy — this is an investment decision as well as an emotional one.

**Imagery:** Use real photos — kids playing, a hosted birthday, the café, parents relaxing. Avoid generic stock where possible. Mobile-first and highly scannable.

## Open decisions (resolve before publishing)

1. **Publish fees publicly?** Showing investment ranges attracts serious enquiries; some brands keep the royalty % for the enquiry conversation. Decide what's public vs. gated.
2. **Investment as ranges vs. "from ₹X".** Ranges are honest; "from" reads more inviting. Pick one and keep it consistent.
3. **Numbers are still indicative.** Investment and any future returns should be finalised once the first branch's economics are validated. Add a light "indicative" note if publishing figures now.
4. **Add social proof later.** Once you have happy customers and early franchise interest, add testimonials and (eventually) a "centres open / coming soon" map.
5. **Trademark.** Register the Play Panda name and logo before promoting franchising publicly.
6. **Lead routing.** Confirm where enquiry-form submissions go (email inbox and/or CRM) and who owns follow-up.
